# Part XXII: What “all drivers” and “all networking” really mean in practice

If your goal is mastery, there are three different levels of knowing a domain.

## Level 1: Architectural knowledge
You know every major subsystem, why it exists, how it fits, and the key abstractions.

## Level 2: Operational knowledge
You can use tools, debug issues, read traces, inspect code paths, and explain behavior.

## Level 3: Implementer knowledge
You can read and write kernel or systems code in that area.

For **drivers**, “all drivers” is not realistic at Level 3, because the Linux kernel supports enormous amounts of hardware. But **all driver frameworks and concepts** absolutely is realistic.

For **networking**, “all networking” is similarly huge, but **all core Linux networking architecture and protocols** is realistic.
